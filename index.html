Research

Format-robust evidence reasoning in LLMs

LLMs resolving knowledge conflicts change their preference when the same evidence is rendered as text, tables or knowledge graphs. FaDe treats format as a nuisance variable and distills format-marginalized teacher targets into a student. In medicine, PMC-ConForm varies evidence source and format independently, and ForMeDER reduces the resulting preference shift on 8 LLMs without external labels.

Self-improving clinical diagnosis agents (ongoing)

Interactive diagnosis agents that take history, order tests and diagnose on MIMIC-IV emergency department cases, and revise their own diagnostic harness from their errors. The question is whether such self-improvement holds on unseen patients.

Spatial intelligence: multimodal and streaming 3D completion

PGNet corrects an image-to-3D shape prior in feature space. CRCNet routes VLM-generated part phrases to vision prototypes for point-wise refinement. BelieveSSC reads the semantic composition of a world model's belief to guide semantic scene completion. CommitStream and the OrbitScan benchmark study the reliability of streaming completion.
