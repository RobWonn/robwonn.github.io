Research

LLM reasoning

Format-robust evidence reasoning

LLMs change which evidence they trust when the same content is shown as text, a table or a knowledge graph. I measure this format bias and remove it by distillation, in general QA and in medicine.

FaDeForMeDERPMC-ConForm
Medical agents · ongoing

Self-improving diagnosis agents

Agents that take history, order tests and diagnose on MIMIC-IV emergency department cases, and revise their own diagnostic harness from their errors. The question is whether the gains hold on unseen patients.

Spatial intelligence

Multimodal and streaming 3D completion

Completing 3D shapes and scenes from partial scans with image, language and world-model priors, and keeping streaming completion consistent over time.

Publications

First-author papers

Format as Nuisance: Intervention-Inspired Cross-Format Distillation for Knowledge Conflicts

Yue Yu*, Wang Luo*, Shenghao Ye, Zepeng Bao, Jiacheng Liu, Yongqi Li, Wenxin Tang, Tieyun Qian

EMNLP 2026OralPaper Award Nomination

Treats evidence format as a nuisance variable and distills format-marginalized teacher targets into a student. Reduces format bias on ConflictBank and two controlled conflict sets across four backbones, with single-pass inference.

Code

Rethinking Multimodal Point Cloud Completion: A Completion-by-Correction Perspective

Wang Luo, Di Wu, Hengyuan Na, Yinlin Zhu, Miao Hu, Guocong Quan

AAAI 2026

Starts from an image-to-3D shape prior and corrects it with the partial scan in feature space, instead of inpainting missing regions. Chamfer Distance −23.5% and F-score +7.1% on ShapeNet-ViPC.

Code

Compose, Route, Complete: Text-Routed Vision Prototypes for Multimodal Point Cloud Completion

Wang Luo, Di Wu, Hengyuan Na, Yinlin Zhu, Shenghao Ye, Miao Hu, Guocong Quan

ACM MM 2026

A VLM writes part-level phrases that compose vision prototypes and route them to points for refinement. Best results on ShapeNet-ViPC; generalizes to unseen categories and KITTI scans.
